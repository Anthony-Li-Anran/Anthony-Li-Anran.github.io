## Website Structure

This website is organized into the following sections:

### Guide
*You are here.* This page provides an overview of the website structure and helps you navigate through different sections.

### CV
My curriculum vitae including:
- **Education**: Academic background
- **Experience**: Internships and student roles
- **Skills**: Technical and academic skills
- **Publications**: Academic publications

### Learning
Learning notes and resources related to my studies and research interests, particularly in:
- Large Language Models (LLM)
- AI Agents
- Data Science
- Related theories and practices

### Projects
Showcase of projects I have worked on, including:
- Academic projects and coursework
- Research projects
- Personal projects and experiments
- Collaborative work

### Publications
A collection of my academic publications, including:
- Journal articles
- Conference papers
- Working papers
- Preprints

Each publication includes details such as venue, date, and citation information.

### Blog Posts
Thoughts, insights, and updates on various topics:
- Research notes and learning progress
- Technical tutorials and what I've learned
- Personal thoughts and reflections
- Academic observations and learnings
- Personal life and hobbies

### Guestbook
A space for visitors to:
- Leave comments and feedback
- Share thoughts
- Ask questions
- Connect with me

Comments are powered by [giscus](https://giscus.app/) and require a GitHub account to participate.

## How to Navigate

- Use the navigation bar at the top to access different sections
- Each section has its own dedicated page with detailed content
- The homepage provides a quick overview of all sections
- Use the search function (if available) to find specific content

## Getting in Touch

If you have any questions or would like to connect, please:
- Leave a message in the [Guestbook](/guestbook/)
- Check my [CV](/cv/) for contact information
- Reach out through social media links in the sidebar

## Technical Details

This website is built with:
- **Jekyll**: Static site generator
- **Academic Pages Template**: Based on Minimal Mistakes theme
- **GitHub Pages**: Hosting platform
- **Markdown**: Content formatting
- **giscus**: Comment system
