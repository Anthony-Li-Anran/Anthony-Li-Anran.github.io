Today marks the birth of my personal website! After careful planning and development, I'm excited to launch this online space where I can showcase my academic work, projects, and learning journey.

- **Projects**: A collection of my technical and research projects

I'll keep updating this site with new stories and experiences as I go along. Feel free to explore and reach out if you'd like to connect!

Thank you for visiting!
